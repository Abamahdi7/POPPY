## Project Status

This project is currently in the early building stage. We are just starting to build the application, and the core features are under active construction.

---

## About The Project

Poppy is an offline desktop application designed to help people focus on real work and stop losing time to digital distractions[cite: 3, 4].

Many people sit in front of a computer for five hours and think they worked hard[cite: 3]. In reality, they often spend less than half of that time doing focused work[cite: 3]. The rest of the time is lost to opening new browser tabs, looking at phones, and taking small breaks that turn into long delays[cite: 3].

Poppy helps solve this problem by making planning simple, helping you start faster, and showing your true progress[cite: 3, 4].

---

## The Problems Poppy Solves

Many people struggle with three common planning problems[cite: 4]:

* **The Action Gap (Hard to Start):** Goals are often too large and unclear[cite: 4]. Poppy helps break big goals into small, clear steps that you can start right away[cite: 4].
* **The Capacity Gap (Planning Too Much):** People often put too many tasks into one day and expect a perfect schedule[cite: 4]. Poppy helps you make realistic plans that match your real energy and available time[cite: 4].
* **The Review Gap (Forgetting to Look Back):** Most people do not compare what they planned with what they actually finished[cite: 4]. Poppy provides a simple review process so you understand your mistakes and do not repeat them[cite: 4].

---

## How It Works

* **Completely Offline:** Poppy runs only on your personal computer[cite: 4]. It does not use cloud servers or internet sync, giving you a quiet workspace away from online distractions[cite: 4].
* **Weekly Planning:** At the start of the week, you select only the tasks you can realistically finish[cite: 4].
* **Weekly Review:** At the end of the week, you check what you finished and what you delayed, giving you an honest look at your work[cite: 4].
* **Purpose First:** Before starting a goal, you write down why it matters to you so you stay motivated[cite: 4].
* **Less Planning, More Action:** Planning should not be a way to avoid real work[cite: 4]. Poppy makes your next step clear so that starting is easy[cite: 4].

---

## Who Is It For?

Poppy is built for anyone who wants to organize their work better[cite: 4]:

* Students learning new skills on their own[cite: 4].
* People working on personal projects[cite: 4].
* Anyone looking for a simple tool that leads to real progress instead of complicated task lists[cite: 4].
